FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I know what size of solar system I need?

It depends on the appliances you want to power and how long you use them. Send us a list of your appliances on WhatsApp and we'll recommend a system at no cost.

Will solar work at night or on cloudy days?

Yes. Batteries store energy during the day so you have power at night, and systems are sized to handle cloudy and rainy-season days.

How long do batteries last?

Lithium batteries typically last much longer than lead-acid options. We'll explain the expected lifespan and warranty for each product before you buy.

Do you install outside Ibadan?

Yes. Our head office is in Akobo, Ibadan, and we've handled projects in Lagos, Abuja, Ogun, Ondo, Ekiti, Kaduna, Imo, Nasarawa and Cross River.

Do you install solar street lights for estates and communities?

Yes. We supply and install solar street lights for estates, roads, schools, religious centres, markets and community projects, including pole installation.

Do you offer warranty and after-sales support?

Yes. Our products come with manufacturer warranties, and we provide maintenance and support after installation.
